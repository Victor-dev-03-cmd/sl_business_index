## NEW direction: Cloudflare Workers AI (not yet live)
- **Recommended model**: `@cf/black-forest-labs/flux-2-klein-4b` (Cloudflare-hosted **FLUX.2 [klein] 4B**). Same model can text-to-image and follow-up image-to-image editing; Black Forest Labs lists **Apache-2.0** commercial-friendly model-weight license.
- Cloudflare official docs: Workers AI Free plan offers **10,000 neurons/day across account**; daily reset 00:00 UTC. This does **NOT** mean 10k images, unlimited commercial generation, guaranteed availability, or free premium third-party image models. On Free, requests over quota fail; never auto-upgrade to Paid.
- Cloudflare endpoint: `POST https://api.cloudflare.com/client/v4/accounts/{account_id}/ai/run/@cf/black-forest-labs/flux-2-klein-4b`. Auth `Bearer {api_token}`. Input is **multipart/form-data even for text-only**: `prompt`, `width`, `height`; image editing includes binary `input_image_0` with dimensions **smaller than 512x512**. Model returns base64 generated image in REST `result.image`; validate bytes and convert to PNG privately. Do not manually construct multipart Content-Type boundary.
- Official docs: https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/get-started/rest-api/ , https://developers.cloudflare.com/changelog/post/2026-01-15-flux-2-klein-4b-workers-ai/ , https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/platform/pricing/ , https://github.com/black-forest-labs/flux2 .
