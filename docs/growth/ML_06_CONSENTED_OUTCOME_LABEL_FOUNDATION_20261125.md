## Critical label interpretation
ML-06 contract labels mean **observed action completion** (listing/contact completeness changed, scheduled reminder manually done, review reply published, campaign draft saved), NOT business success or causal recommendation effectiveness. `not_completed` is only legitimate when the complete observation window and source coverage are positively evidenced; missing event is **UNKNOWN**. Historical logs, lead form submissions and vendor-entered `converted` cannot retrospectively prove sales or causal growth. Future real labels additionally require decision-exposure time, time-matched baselines and confounder treatment.
