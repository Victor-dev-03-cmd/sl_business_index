Retry must recheck revocation, entitlement, approval and budget; verify idempotency and uncertainty, not blind repeats. Define no-action/abstain fallback.
- Prefer PostgreSQL-native queue (pgmq/Supabase Queues candidate) and scheduled dispatch after documentation/version/cost evaluation. No long-running browser job, unbounded serverless task, extra Redis or microservices until measured need. Jobs have tenant/business, immutable action ID, schedule, state, attempts, lease/timeout, receipt/error and retry policy. Use outbox or transactional job creation for critical transitions. Protect external tokens; KMS/encryption, OAuth state/PKCE where supported, capability scopes, refresh + revoke, signed webhooks, quiet hours, consent/suppression and kill switch. No password scraping or personal-account automation.
- AI: deterministic SQL/rules first, retrieval/RAG over authorized verified context, structured outputs/typed tool calls, LLM provider adapter with per-tenant/global budget, validation, abstention and audit. Existing Gemini voice and Puter QA remain unchanged until approved. No arbitrary SQL/tool permissions for model. Treat retrieved business/vendor/provider content as untrusted; do not let it change policy or bypass approvals. Separate draft/read actions from writes/outbound/cost actions, show exact scope, cost and consequence. No private customer data to third-party model unless lawful, purpose-limited, approved.
- Original proprietary portfolio: SLBI-GeoRank (existing baseline, later evaluated LTR); SLBI-GrowthScore (rules/evidence); SLBI-SEOScore (technical evidence); SLBI-CampaignIQ (templates and seasonality -> A/B); SLBI-LeadIQ (rules -> validated scoring); SLBI-SecurityScore (config checks -> anomaly detection); SLBI-Recommend (context/category -> evaluated retrieval); SLBI-NeuralCore (model registry/evaluation). Establish dataset splits, ground truth, leakage safeguards, multilingual and subgroup quality, calibration, latency, costs, failure/rollback thresholds.
