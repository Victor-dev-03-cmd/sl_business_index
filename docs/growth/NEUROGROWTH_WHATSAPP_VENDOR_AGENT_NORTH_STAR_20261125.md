# NeuroGrowth — WhatsApp-first Vendor AI Operator (Product North Star)
**Decision recorded:** 2026-11-25. **Status:** approved product vision and implementation contract / research DESIGN ONLY. **Not a shipped WhatsApp bot or unrestricted API access.** This document preserves the vendor owner's requirement across GrowthOS ML-07+, G1–G9 and Marketing Studio.

## Non-negotiable owner intent
A vendor should be able to speak or send **one short Tamil, Sinhala or English request via WhatsApp**, and have their own secure NeuroGrowth assistant carry out end-to-end preparation across **their own** SLBusinessIndex tools: Business Profile, Settings, Marketing Studio, poster generator, caption lab, campaign library/calendar, connected social media, email marketing, CRM, analytics and reports. **The vendor should not need to open the dashboard for routine planning, approvals or summaries**. It remains an optional visual control centre. Agent creates drafts/previews first, sends the actual poster/image and caption for WhatsApp approval, executes *only exact approved items*, and reports receipts/status via WhatsApp or owner dashboard.

**Illustrative journey:** vendor: “Christmas is five days away. Prepare a promotion: poster, Instagram/Facebook captions, social schedule and an email campaign.” Agent checks local calendar and clarifies dates when the statement is inconsistent (e.g. on 2026-11-25 Christmas 2026 is December 25, not five days away); reads **server-authenticated owner-scoped** brand assets, current marketing entitlements and connected providers; prepares coordinated draft(s) in existing Marketing Studio, voice/text reply with image preview and precise channel-specific descriptions; proposes email subject/body + consented recipient count without disclosing addresses; asks for edit/approval. User may say “Change headline to Tamil; use orange; do not send email yet”; agent re-renders and invalidates old approvals. Then vendor approves exact poster/caption/channel/time and email subject/content/recipient segment and capped cost. Agent performs only approved provider actions, verifies delivery/post IDs and reports successes, failures, pending jobs, spend and source facts; “send summary” works in WhatsApp, dashboard can show the same audit history.

## Principle: broad tool discoverability, narrow authority
The agent SHOULD be able to **understand and navigate all vendor-owned modules** through a unified tool registry, but NEVER receive blanket database, admin or account authority. Permissions **per business, tool, field, provider, action, resource, time and quota** are independently enforced by trusted services. Vendor Chat/WhatsApp content and model proposals are untrusted input, NOT permission. These are different concepts:
- **Read**: scoped, minimized authenticated facts (business profile, public campaign history, permitted aggregate analytics, status/connected provider metadata, permitted Settings fields). Only after verified owner + business RLS; never service-role or cross-tenant.
- **Prepare**: create/update *private draft* campaign, AI poster, captions, permitted email draft, content calendar and owner-visible worklist; must check owner permissions and entitlement. Drafting into durable private storage is a write and requires an owner-authorized scope, even if no publish consent.
- **Preview**: send expiring, privacy-filtered image/media and per-channel caption **to the verified owner WhatsApp chat**, with accessible text alternative; never leak third-party customer contact details.
- **Publish/send/modify**: high-consequence external publication, emailing, scheduled campaign, sending customer messages, modifying business Settings, deleting data, pricing or paid ad spend require *fresh exact-action approval, appropriate provider OAuth, transactional outbox, audit and idempotency*. Never allow a generic “you can access everything” toggle to bypass separate sensitive-action policies.
- **Prohibited or human-only**: subscription/billing changes, factory reset/data deletes, payout, verified badge, security recovery, privilege escalation and arbitrary account transfer remain blocked. Some Settings may allow proposal/review, never silent permission change.

## A single WhatsApp request → safe multi-step orchestration
1. **WhatsApp identity and onboarding**: Meta business/account eligibility, officially authorized Cloud API setup, approved business number and webhook. Owner starts a short authenticated link flow from the signed-in SLBI dashboard (expiring one-time challenge and step-up verification as needed). Bind a Meta business-scoped contact identity + verified WhatsApp conversation to a specific SLBI user ID and approved business ID(s), with revocation, device loss procedures, multiple-business selection and sender spoofing resistance. A phone number alone is **not** account identity. May use an SLBI managed service number or provider-eligible business number, **subject to Meta-supported onboarding and policy**; do not assume existing ordinary WhatsApp app number can always be co-used with API.
2. **Request capture**: signature-verified, deduplicated Meta webhook; encrypted-at-rest minimal prompt; user-initiated Tamil/Sinhala/English voice transcription where lawful/consented; no silent transcription of third-party messages. Server resolves owner/business and conversation window. Reject foreign users/ineligible business.
3. **Planner**: multilingual LLM proposes a *structured plan*, not arbitrary code; read-only tool registry snapshots current owned Brand Profile, G1/G2/G3/G5 facts, Settings safety metadata, exact social/email provider connections, campaign entitlements, calendar and limits. Verify dates, offer terms, locales and pricing; no invented seasonal dates, discounts, consent or recipient counts.
4. **Skill/tool execution**: use existing Marketing Studio workflows (AI poster, caption lab, campaign draft, saved library, content calendar) with explicit bounded typed tools. Reuse rather than fork existing production pages, Supabase RLS and marketing state. Our PyTorch action-ranking NeuroGrowth model and multilingual planner are separate; numeric model *cannot* authorize tools.
5. **Approval manifest**: immutable `plan_id`, `plan_revision`, `business_id`, scoped provider/account IDs, poster asset/version hash, per-channel caption hash, email subject/content hash, exact consented audience SEGMENT ID and estimated audience count, schedule/time zone, provider permissions/remaining limits, unit and total spend cap, expiry, user ID + WhatsApp verified identity, and requested operations. Delivery to owner's WhatsApp with image and readable list. Give **Approve this item**, **Edit**, **Skip**, **Cancel** and a **Final review**; WhatsApp interactive buttons or human text interpreted as intent; the backend always verifies a fresh revision and action-specific approval. A final batch approval may authorize a **displayed immutable manifest** of pre-reviewed actions, never unseen side effects. Provider token refresh or changes invalidate unsuitable approvals.
6. **Execute**: durable queue/outbox per action with atomically consumed approval nonce, replay/duplicate-message guards, least-privilege per-provider OAuth, bounded fanout, retries with idempotency, recipient suppression before sending, provider budgets/quota/cost ceilings, rate limits and cancellation where possible. Approvals for sending email cannot be assumed from a social-post approval. No inferred or imported customer contacts without verified opt-in or lawful basis.
7. **Summary**: use actual provider statuses / IDs only: **drafted / approved / queued / published / delivered / partially failed / needs reconnection / cancelled**. “Successfully posted” only after provider confirms, not API request initiation; no fabricated revenue, engagement, customer conversion. Show concise owner WhatsApp summary with links to optional dashboard audit, expense, retry and failed-channel actions.

## High-risk WhatsApp platform/policy gate — DO NOT BYPASS
- WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy requires recipient opt-in, respect of opt-out, and approved message templates for business-initiated conversations; outside the 24-hour customer-service window, **only approved templates** are allowed. Image previews and interactive buttons depend on actual approved API capabilities and conversation/template context. See official: https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/ and https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/overview/ (review policy versions again at implementation).
- **Meta WhatsApp Business Solution Terms (March 6, 2026)** restrict AI Providers from using WhatsApp as a **primary channel for general-purpose AI assistants** (some region-specific exceptions): https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/business-solution-terms/ . Prior to onboarding Sri Lankan vendors, obtain current Meta platform/policy eligibility confirmation for the proposed **business-specific commerce/operations workflow**. Distinguish service-specific marketing automation **ancillary to SLBI's business service** from distributing a general-purpose AI chatbot; classification is Meta's, not ours. If forbidden/unapproved, use **dashboard/web inbox** and permitted outbound templates/notifications instead, NOT unofficial WhatsApp automation or reverse-engineered APIs.
- Official Meta Tech Provider / business onboarding, number eligibility, app review/permissions, verified webhook and service-human escalation must be satisfied. Not all social connectors/accounts support automatic publishing. No claim that existing Gmail/Meta OAuth flows or email outbox are production-live until separately verified.
- Email outreach: sender domain or delegated mailbox connected legitimately, validated recipients only, current opt-in & suppression/unsubscribe, verified delivery provider, anti-spam compliance, business opt-out and future data deletion. **Vendor approval to send is not recipient consent**.
- Agent action traces and approval history must be minimal, access-controlled, retained to approved policy, with secrets vaulted, no provider access tokens / raw customer messages in LLM context or WhatsApp summaries.
